Privacy Policy — Shutterstorm

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how FirstCom Studio ("we", "us") handles information when you use the game Shutterstorm (com.firstcom.hailstorm) on iOS and Android.

1. No account, no server

Shutterstorm does not ask you to create an account and does not operate a server of its own. We never receive your name, e-mail address, or any other information that identifies you. The app reaches beyond your device only through ads and Firebase (sections 3 and 4).

2. Data stored on your device

The app keeps its progress in one file in the app's local storage (save.json):

  • Your chase grade and the lens you chose.
  • Your storm atlas — the best photo of each scene. Photos are kept as the in-game data needed to draw them again, not as image files, and they come only from the game itself; the app does not use your camera or photo library.
  • Today's assignment and the stamps you have collected.
  • Your settings, and whether you have opened the app before.

This data is not sent to us and is not backed up to any cloud service by the app. Deleting the app deletes it.

3. Advertising

Shutterstorm is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob — a single banner at the bottom of the menu screens, never while you are out chasing a storm. To serve and measure ads, Google's SDK collects data such as your device's advertising identifier, device and OS information, IP address, ad interaction events, and the SDK's own diagnostic data (crash logs and performance data). This collection is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

  • iOS — the app asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency) before ads initialise. If you decline, ads still show but are not personalised using the advertising identifier. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android — you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads.
  • EEA / UK / Switzerland and some US states — a consent dialog (Google User Messaging Platform) appears before ads load where the law requires it; you can accept or decline personalised ads there.

4. Crash reporting and usage analytics

To find and fix crashes and to understand how the app is used (for example, how many people install it and whether they come back), Shutterstorm includes two Google Firebase services. We do not use either to identify you.

  • Firebase Crashlytics — when the app crashes or hits an error, it sends the stack trace, the app's state at the time, device and OS information, and a random installation identifier used to count how many users a crash affects.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — records app usage events such as first open, sessions, screens viewed and ads shown. They are sent with a random app-instance identifier, device and OS information, and an approximate location derived from a masked IP address. On iOS the advertising identifier is included only if you allow tracking; on Android it follows your advertising ID settings above.

This data is processed by Google under the Firebase privacy and security terms and Google's Privacy Policy. The progress stored on your device (section 2) is not part of it.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or transfer your data.
  • The only third-party SDKs in the app are Google AdMob (section 3) and Google Firebase Crashlytics and Analytics (section 4). The app does not use Game Center, Google Play Games, cloud backup, or in-app purchases.
  • We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or any other device content.

6. Children's Privacy

Shutterstorm is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction you may have the right to access, correct, or delete data held about you. Data stored by the app lives only on your device and is removed when you delete the app. For data held by Google in connection with advertising, crash reporting, or analytics, see My Ad Center and the device settings above. For anything else, contact us at the address below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
