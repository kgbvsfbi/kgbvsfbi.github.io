How it plays

Modes

01

Daily

Tessera #N — the same puzzle for everyone. Easy on Monday, hard on Sunday, and every Friday a Special that bends one rule. Missed a day? It waits in the archive.

02

Grades

Three board sizes with five grades each, from a quick break to a long think.

03

Pictures

Solve a few boards and a picture paints itself in. 5,000 pictures included, more arriving every month.

04

Versus

Race an AI, or up to 4 players nearby over Bluetooth on the same board.

Also in the app

One answer, never a guess

Every puzzle is proven by a solver to have exactly one solution before it reaches you.

Works and records

Finished pictures and framed collections, stamps, best and average times, and your daily time on the leaderboard.

Yours to play

Hints and undo, light and dark themes, and it plays offline. Share your result — no spoilers, just the number and the time.
